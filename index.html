Credit discipline first, then the systems behind it

I started on the recovery desk of a hire purchase company, where every overdue installment had a name, a branch and a deadline. That taught me collections as a daily operating rhythm, not a month-end surprise.

Over 8.5 years at RD (Ruba Digital), a 160+ branch hire-purchase company, I moved from recovery into credit policy and then senior Accounts Receivable: running daily Due/OD achievement reporting in Excel, automating performance tracking for 600+ sales reps and leading a forensic review that uncovered PKR 17M (USD 75K) in aging defects.

At Wonder Bakery in Dubai I took over AR for 15,000+ invoices a month, reconciled customer statements, supported the move to Microsoft Dynamics and later stepped up to Project Accountant, running the full books of a 40+ outlet retail project and its UAE e-invoicing rollout.

I sit between finance, operations and IT: I understand the ledger and the system that feeds it.
